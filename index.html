About Me

A licensed Architect who treats every drawing like construction depends on it.

I'm Orlando Argueta — a licensed Architect based in Tegucigalpa, Honduras, with three years of professional experience in as-built survey documentation, Scan to BIM workflows, project management, and construction administration. My work has supported A&E firms across the United States, including projects with Trileaf Corporation and national brands in banking, retail, food service, and specialty environments.

What sets my work apart isn't just speed — it's the architectural eye behind every line. I don't just trace point clouds; I read them. I understand how walls connect, where mechanical systems route, and what your contractor will actually need on-site. Operating on Central Standard Time means real-time collaboration with your team — same business hours, direct line of contact, and drawings that ship with fewer revisions and fewer surprises.
