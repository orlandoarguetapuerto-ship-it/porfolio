How It Works

Step 01
Get in touch
Reach out by email or LinkedIn with a brief description of your project. We'll respond quickly to confirm scope and availability.
Step 02
Receive a proposal
You'll receive a clear proposal outlining deliverables, timeline, and pricing. No surprises — just a straightforward agreement.
Step 03
50% deposit to start
A 50% deposit secures your place in the queue and kicks off the project. Established clients may qualify for NET 15/30 terms.
Step 04
Send us the data
Provide the point cloud (Matterport or NavVis), field sketches, and site photographs taken by your surveyor. The 4-business-day clock starts once all materials are confirmed received.
Step 05
Drafting & delivery
We produce your drawings and deliver the PDF and DWG files within 4 business days of receiving complete data.
Step 06
Review period
You have up to 4 business days to review the deliverables, with up to 2 revision rounds included. After this period, the project is considered complete.
Step 07
Final payment
Once the review period closes and the project is complete, the remaining 50% is due.

Why Work With Me

From survey to final plans, I help you deliver accurate, construction-ready drawings faster and with fewer revisions.

I'm a licensed Architect with a university degree in Architecture and 3 years of professional experience spanning as-built survey documentation, project management, architectural design, and construction administration. My experience includes work with Trileaf Corporation and documentation for national brands across the U.S.
